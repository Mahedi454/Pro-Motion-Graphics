# Cinematic Premium Educational Visual System

## Art direction
Aim for a premium documentary-meets-editorial explainer: intentional composition, dimensional depth, confident typography, precise diagrams, restrained atmosphere, and purposeful camera language.

## Composition
- Use a consistent grid and generous negative space.
- One dominant focal point per scene.
- Separate background, content, and foreground layers.
- Maintain safe margins for titles and captions.
- Use asymmetry when it improves visual tension, but preserve balance.
- Use scale and framing to communicate importance.

## Palette
Start with a deep navy/charcoal base. Choose one primary accent and one secondary highlight based on subject and brand. Use semantic colors consistently for categories or process stages. Maintain contrast; never rely on hue alone.

## Typography
- Maximum two primary font families.
- Clear hierarchy: hero, section, body, annotation, caption.
- Use short on-screen copy.
- Avoid tiny labels, thin weights, long all-caps passages, and decorative type for technical content.
- Test at mobile playback size.

## Cinematic motion
- Camera push/pull: emphasize discovery or reveal.
- Pan: follow a process or connect related elements.
- Parallax: create depth only when it does not distract.
- Mask reveal: introduce a diagram or chapter.
- Morph/transform: show conceptual change when the relationship is valid.
- Stagger: establish reading order.
- Hold: allow the viewer to read and think.

Use easing and spring dynamics deliberately. Avoid constant linear motion, uncontrolled bounce, random camera movement, gratuitous zooms, excessive glow, particle storms, and transitions that interrupt learning.

## Lighting and atmosphere
Use subtle gradients, vignettes, shadows, grain, or ambient particles only where they support mood and hierarchy. Do not obscure text or diagrams.

## Visual vocabulary
Choose the most explanatory form:
- Concept maps and node diagrams
- Flowcharts and pipelines
- Timelines and maps
- Comparisons and before/after
- Equations and progressive derivations
- Charts with labeled axes and units
- Code panels and state diagrams
- Custom vector illustrations
- Layered spatial metaphors

## Responsive composition
Design each aspect ratio deliberately. Re-layout vertical content for 9:16. Keep the subject and labels legible on mobile.
