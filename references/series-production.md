# Educational Series Production

For a multi-episode series, create a series bible before episode production.

## Series bible
- Audience and learning pathway
- Episode sequence and prerequisites
- Consistent intro/outro rules
- Typography, palette, diagram language
- Reusable scene patterns
- Narration tone and terminology
- Caption conventions
- Sound identity
- Source and citation conventions

## Episode workflow
1. Define the episode objective and prerequisites.
2. Research and verify episode-specific claims.
3. Draft script and storyboard.
4. Reuse shared components without making every episode visually identical.
5. Track recurring terms, examples, and continuity.
6. Review the episode independently and as part of the series.
7. Maintain an episode status and asset register.

## Avoid
- Repeating the same hook format every time.
- Inconsistent definitions between episodes.
- Visual callbacks that assume viewers remember details without a reminder.
- Reusing an example in a way that confuses the new concept.
