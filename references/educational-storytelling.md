# Educational Storytelling

## Principle
Teach a mental model, not a sequence of attractive slides. Every scene must answer a question or advance the explanation.

## Story arc
1. Hook: question, puzzle, conflict, surprising observation.
2. Promise: learning outcome.
3. Context: relevance and prerequisites.
4. Explain: definitions and mechanism in logical order.
5. Demonstrate: worked example or case.
6. Test: misconception or quick knowledge check.
7. Recap: compact visual summary.
8. Close: takeaway and next step.

## Topic adaptation
- Mathematics: progressive equation builds, annotated variables, number lines, geometric constructions, worked calculations.
- Computer science: data flow, state changes, code highlighting, architecture diagrams, terminal simulations.
- AI/ML: model pipelines, tensors, decision boundaries, loss curves, training loops, carefully labeled examples.
- Science: physical processes, scale comparisons, causal diagrams, labeled models, units.
- History: timelines, maps, primary-source excerpts, cause-and-effect sequences; distinguish fact from interpretation.
- Business/economics: systems diagrams, scenarios, process maps, data visualizations; identify assumptions.
- Literature/books: themes, character relationships, argument maps, contextualized quotations within allowed limits.
- Health/medicine: cautious language, reliable sources, no personalized diagnosis; explain uncertainty and encourage qualified care where appropriate.
- Law/policy: jurisdiction and date matter; distinguish general information from legal advice.
- Tutorials: goal, prerequisites, setup, steps, expected result, common errors, recap.

## Technical teaching
Introduce every symbol before use. Show intermediate calculations. Label units. Keep rounding consistent. For algorithms, show input → operation → intermediate state → output. Never skip a step the learner needs to reproduce the result.

## Pacing
Hold dense diagrams long enough to understand. Use a visual reset between major concepts. Avoid fast kinetic typography for material that requires careful reading.
