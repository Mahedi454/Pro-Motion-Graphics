# Audio, Voiceover, and Sound Design

## Narration
Narration is the instructional spine. Use clear pacing, natural emphasis, correct pronunciation, and intentional pauses. Preserve user-provided narration unless editing is requested.

## Audio hierarchy
1. Voiceover
2. Music bed
3. Sound effects

Keep voice intelligible. Music should support emotion without competing. SFX reinforce every meaningful motion event (each kind of motion has its own sound, quietly mixed) and never mask the voice; do not add sound to purely ambient motion such as the drifting background.

## Rights
Use user-owned, original, public-domain, or appropriately licensed audio. Record source and license details. Do not imitate a living artist or reproduce recognizable copyrighted music.

## Cue sheet
For each cue record:
- Scene/time
- Asset filename
- Purpose
- Entry/exit and fade
- Relative level
- Source/license

The built-in sound effects are included in the starter. If narration or music is missing, create a cue plan for it and label it as a plan, not as included audio.

## Motion sound effects
Sound effects tied to motion are defined in `references/sound-design-system.md`: different sounds for different kinds of motion, with rotating variants. The starter ships an original synthesized library, so no sound licences are needed for the built-in effects.
